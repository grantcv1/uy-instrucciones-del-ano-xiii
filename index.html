The Instrucciones del Año XIII in Akoma Ntoso

The instructions José Artigas gave to the deputies of the Banda Oriental on April 13, 1813, for the Assembly in Buenos Aires. In twenty articles they ask for independence, a confederation, the separation of powers and a republican government. They are marked up in Akoma Ntoso, the OASIS standard for legal and legislative documents. The text is in Spanish.

How it was made

The XML was generated by Claude for Grant Vergottini from the Archivo Artigas, which prints the copy authenticated by Artigas and held by the Museo Histórico Nacional. Claude didn’t write a word of this text. It keeps the spelling of that printing. The painting is by Diógenes Hequet, and shows Artigas dictating to his secretary.
